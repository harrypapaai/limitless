## 📖 User Guide

### Getting Started

When you first open the application, you will see the main dashboard. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the layout. You can create contracts or view existing ones from the menu.

### Creating a Contract

1. Click on the "Create Contract" button.
2. Fill in the necessary details.
3. Click "Submit" to deploy your contract.

### Tracking Transactions

To track your transactions, navigate to the "Transactions" tab. Here, you can see a history of all your actions.
